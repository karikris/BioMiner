# Papilio demoleus statistical audit

The audit status is `insufficient_sample`. No real human-reviewed Flickr label
artifact is present, so precision, recall, false-positive rate and competitor
confusion remain unavailable rather than being invented or treated as zero.

A deterministic, outcome-blind representative queue of 50 records was selected
from the real 18,041-record Papilio sampling frame. Its sampling weights expand
back to the full population. These records now require source-bound human
review before species-quality metrics can be calculated.

Reference escalation is deferred until those labels exist. Statistical evidence
may prioritize reference review, but neither missing metrics nor a future flag
would prove reference identity.
